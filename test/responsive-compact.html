Targeted by e2e/responsive-compact.spec.ts. Inline 2-month range picker with compact-below="520": when the element's own box drops below 520px it collapses to one month and hides the Today/Clear buttons (keyed on the element box via core's ResizeObserver, not the viewport). A second picker with no compact-below is the control — it never compacts.

dark-mode contrast

Targeted by e2e/dark-mode.spec.ts. A daterangepicker themed for a dark page via the documented --base-* override set. The specs verify that interactive states (day hover, focus, selected) maintain readable text-vs-background contrast on a dark surface. Each picker is pinned to initial-date="2026-06-15" so day cells are deterministic.

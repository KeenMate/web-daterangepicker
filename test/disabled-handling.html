disabledDatesHandling strategies

Targeted by e2e/disabled-handling.spec.ts. All pickers share initial-date 2026-06-15 and have 2026-06-12 + 2026-06-13 configured as disabled dates (the "weekend gap" the user must pick around / through).

'allow' (default) — range spans over disabled days
'prevent' — selection crossing a disabled date is rejected
'block' — range snaps to last enabled day before the gap
highlight-disabled-in-range="false" — disabled days inside a range are not highlighted
